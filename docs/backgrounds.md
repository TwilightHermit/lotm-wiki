# Backgrounds

Your background describes where your character came from, before the Pathways and the Sequences. Each background gives skill proficiencies, tool proficiencies, languages, equipment, and one special feature.

## Acolyte

*You served the Church of the Evernight Goddess, tending to its rituals and its quiet watch over the night.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Religion

**Tool Proficiencies:** None

**Languages:** Two of your choice

**Equipment:** A holy symbol, a prayer book, five sticks of incense, vestments, a set of common clothes, and a pouch containing 15 gp.

### Feature: Shelter of the Faithful

As an acolyte, you command the respect of those who share your faith. You and your companions can receive free healing and care at a church of your faith, and you can call on its clergy for modest help.
