# Hermes

During the Fourth Epoch, Ancient Hermes was simplified into the current recognized form, making it easier to be used, taught, and disseminated for daily use.

However, it no longer had such a strong and direct effect on rituals. It is improved to contemporary Hermes.

For Beyonders, Hermes language is the language that must be mastered. It is universally used by denizens of the Spirit World, even if their origins in life were different.
